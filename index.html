📝 a note

Naira, I don't really know why I'm writing this. Maybe because some nights still feel a little too quiet, and somehow my mind always ends up back to you.

I still remember all the random things we used to do. The dumb jokes that only we found funny, the late-night talks when neither of us wanted to sleep yet. And honestly I miss that sometimes.

Not even in a "please come back" kind of way. I think I just miss the feeling of having someone who made everything feel lighter without even trying. You did that a lot, probably without realizing it.

I know things changed. People change too, I guess. Maybe we were only meant to know each other for that part of life. Still... I'm grateful it happened at all.

So this is just me saying thank you. For the memories, for the comfort, for being someone I could genuinely smile with. Some people leave quietly but still stay in certain parts of you. I think you'll always be one of those people for me.

I hope you're okay now. I hope life's softer with you these days.

And if someday you randomly remember me too, I hope it makes you smile a little.

— Kak Deedat
